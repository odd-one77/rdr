How matching works

People
Matched on the unique ID when there is one, otherwise on name and phone. A matching name with a different phone, or the other way round, is flagged as a possible duplicate. You choose: update, create new, or skip.
Rows without a name
Kept under their position or role name, with the Bolero ID and faceplate colour. A name repeated with another ID becomes a second entry.
Devices
Matched on SI number. An import never changes a device's status or who holds it.
Types from a delivery note
Read from the description: BL-BPK is a Bolero Beltpack. PRO-D1, PRO-D2, AIR-D1, MAX-E1 and Covert are headsets. HC Headset Cord is a Radio Headset Adapter. Hytera and Motorola are radios. Panels, antennas, power, batteries, chargers, cables and cases are listed as ignored.
Bolero ID
Comes in with the allocation. A pack labelled or reserved for a person is linked to that ID, and a pack scanned out to them shows under it until it comes back.
